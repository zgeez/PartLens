# PartLens visual identity

Original project assets created using the built-in image-generation tool for the owner's interface request.
The source is an original lens/circuit concept; no existing chat-app mark, vendor logo or private board is used.
Selected outputs are saved as `logo.png` and `hero.png`. Small PNG variants and the Windows icon are
format/size derivatives preserving their transparency; they are not separate engineering illustrations.
These are decorative graphics, never circuit evidence. Generated artwork is distinct from manufacturer
documentation and does not imply a validated design or sponsor endorsement.

## Original logo prompt (built-in mode; superseded)

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original application logo mark for PartLens, an electronics engineering chat assistant.
Primary request: Design one polished, distinctive, minimal symbol that unites an optical lens and an electronic circuit. A softly squared open ring with a precise inner aperture and two short circuit terminals, forming a subtle abstract P without a literal letter. Elegant geometry, calm proportions, substantial strokes that remain readable as a small desktop icon. Restrained deep teal and soft jade, with a very subtle satin depth. Centered standalone mark with generous clear space.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background.
Style/medium: premium contemporary software identity, crisp vector-like edges, simple silhouette, no busy detail.
Constraints: original design, no text, no wordmark, no watermark, no imitation of ChatGPT, Claude, or any existing company logo; no mockup device, no white background.

## Current Part + Lens mark (built-in edit mode)

The owner's clarification requested a symbol that defines the name PartLens. The selected mark combines
an electronic component (Part) inside a handheld magnifying glass (Lens). It is used by `logo.png`,
the small PNG variants and the executable's seven-size `PartLens.ico`. Earlier versions are preserved
in ignored local revision/build storage. These images remain decorative branding, not engineering evidence.

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand
Asset type: PartLens logo and Windows application icon.
Edit target: supplied teal magnifying-glass mark.
Primary request: Make this unmistakably express the NAME PARTLENS: an electronic PART being examined through a magnifying LENS. Keep the clear magnifying-glass outer silhouette and diagonal handle, but replace the empty lens interior with one large, simplified, clearly recognizable electronic integrated-circuit component seen through the glass. A dark-teal square chip with a pale jade central die and three bold short pins per side, centered inside the circular lens. The chip occupies about two thirds of the glass diameter, creating a strong readable chip-within-magnifier symbol. One delicate glass highlight only, do not obscure the component. The handle's restrained circuit trace ties the two meanings together.
Design: original premium desktop software mark, simplified confident geometry and restrained satin depth, deep teal and jade palette. Strong contrast, clean silhouette, recognizable at 16–32 pixels; no tiny circuit clutter.
Invariants: retain magnifying glass rather than camera optics, preserve transparent background, preserve teal/jade identity, centered icon with modest padding. No aperture, iris, camera, shutter blades, extra magnifiers, text, letters, wordmark, watermark, square app tile, real board data or existing company logo. Actual alpha transparency outside the mark.
